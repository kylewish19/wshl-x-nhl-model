# ATG basis-stratified prospective evaluation
Date: 2026-11-26. Research-only diagnostics; official v0.2.0 unchanged.

The matched ATG pregame runner preserves the official model_basis value from the frozen core candidate board in probabilities.csv and matched_prices.csv. The nested manifest records counts by basis; the grading loader verifies the two files' labels and counts in addition to existing hashes and upload cutoff checks. No outcome or price determines membership.

Groups: limited_history (limited_history_fallback prefix); established_anchor (transition_ml_anchor); other_or_unknown (all other/missing labels, never silently pooled with anchors). Every matched candidate belongs to exactly one group. All three existing models are compared on the same player IDs, odds and participation outcomes. Existing qualifying flags and paper profit fields are read without reselection; DNP voids remain voids. Report counts, realized and expected scorers, Brier/log loss/AUC and existing paper price performance; no new selection or new hybrid model. Cumulative rates and probability scores are observation-weighted; paper ROI uses settled stakes only. AUC is per-slate, not averaged cumulatively. The other/unknown category remains visible even when empty.

The unified grader emits atg_basis_graded.csv, atg_basis_summary.csv, atg_basis_ledger.csv and atg_basis_cumulative.csv for new locks with basis labels. Review and commit the ledger separately from the official and existing prospective ledgers. Duplicate date/group/model keys fail. Replays never append to the ledger. Earlier immutable locks without labels remain gradeable through their original summary; their basis labels can be independently joined to the exact hash-bound core candidate file. The Nov25 baseline uses FROZEN_SOURCE_SUBGROUP_REVIEW: original predictions and price decisions were pregame, but the subgroup reporting was added after outcomes. It stays distinct from future CLEAN_SHADOW_PREGAME subgroup locks. No grouping hindsight is counted as new independent validation.

Existing research/official records are untouched. No new historical raw data, training, price threshold, promotion or sportsbook action. Advanced data permissions and private artifact backup remain unresolved.
